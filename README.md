# schrod
A derivation of the Schrödinger equation

Many posts about the Schrödinger equation appeared (May 2026) in my LinkedIn feed.
The readers who already know about this equation do not learn anything
while those who do not know will not likely learn something, assuming they would want to.
The sole explanation of the meaning of each symbol is not sufficient to build a mental image of its meaning.

The exposed derivation of the Schrödinger equation might be considered as a very naive justication for the mathematical 
formulation of one of the pillars of quantum mechanics, proposed by someone who is not a real physicist.
